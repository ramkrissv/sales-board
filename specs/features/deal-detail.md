# Feature: Deal Detail Hub

## Summary
13-tab modal showing complete deal context with AI features embedded.

## Tabs
1. Details — core deal info, edit-in-place
2. Stakeholders — team mapping, decision maker flags
3. Tasks — pipeline tasks with priority
4. Meetings — transcript upload, AI intelligence extraction
5. Email — AI email composer with A/B variants
6. Competitive — battle station, competitive intel
7. Health — client health score, risk signals
8. Workshop — linked assessment, create on demand
9. Log — activity feed
10. Documents — SOW preview, contracts
11. Pricing — rate cards
12. Presales — proposal preview
13. Contracts — linked contracts

## AI Features Embedded
DealCopilot, MeetingIntelligence, WinLossAutopsy, AIEmailComposer, ClientHealthScore, CompetitiveBattleStation, AIDealRoom, WorkflowRunner
